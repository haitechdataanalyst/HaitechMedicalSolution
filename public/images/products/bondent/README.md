# `bondent/` — Bondent products

Folders mirror the site's category URLs: `endo-files/<type>/`, `endo-device/<area>/` and `imaging/`.
A photo sits in the category of the product it shows, found from `data/products.json`.

`uncatalogued/` holds 30 photos of Bondent products (orthodontics, microscopes, implants and similar) that do not have a page on the site yet. When a product is added, move its photo into the matching category folder.
